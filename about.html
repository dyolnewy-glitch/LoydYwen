About Me

Hi! I'm Loyd Ywen Masangcay. I was born on February 26, 2003, and I am from Nancalobasaan, Urdaneta City, Pangasinan. I am a passionate and creative individual who enjoys exploring digital technology and creating meaningful designs.

I am a graduate of Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) from Urdaneta City University (UCU), where I completed my degree on June 24, 2026. Throughout my academic journey, I developed skills in web design, UI/UX design, graphic design, and different digital technologies.

During my On-the-Job Training (OJT) at the LGU Urdaneta City – City Library Department, I gained experience in data encoding, organizing digital records, and assisting with library information management.

I have a strong interest in Graphic Design, Web Development, and Digital Creativity. I enjoy editing photos and videos, creating visual designs, and continuously learning new skills that help me grow as an IT professional.

My goal is to become a skilled professional who combines technology and creativity to create useful and visually engaging digital solutions.
